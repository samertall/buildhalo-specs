## Inspiration
Traditional assembly instructions require repeated switches between the object and a manual or phone. BuildHalo proposes placing visual guidance directly over the task.

## What we are designing
A spatial assembly assistant for Snap SPECS. The focused task is a tabletop phone stand. A virtual base, upright and cradle show where components go, while animated ghost geometry clarifies motion. Simple pinch controls propose a repeatable, accessible three-step flow.

## Design decisions
Use contextual spatial placement, a limited number of large targets, progressive disclosure, and readable text inside a narrow AR field of view. The planned Lens uses a simulated workbench instead of claiming unsupported automatic real-object recognition.

## What has actually been built
A browser-based interactive concept storyboard, a candidate design brief, CLAD implementation handoff, and test plan. **An official plan-lens-generated brief, functional CLAD-built Lens Studio project, and LEAF test results do not yet exist here.**

## Challenges
Fitting useful instructions within the glasses' field of view while keeping hands available and attention on the task. The design also must be testable in the computer preview without assuming live camera vision.
